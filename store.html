Safety, honestly.

Reclaimed lithium-ion batteries carry real risks if handled carelessly — we won't pretend otherwise. Here's what "safe reuse" would actually mean before a single unit is ever sold:

  • Every cell tested individually. Capacity, internal resistance and physical inspection — cells that don't pass go to certified battery recycling, not into products.
  • Independent certification before sale. No product ships without third-party battery-safety testing (e.g. IEC 62133) and the compliance any new power bank requires.
  • Clear labelling, always. Every buyer will know the cells are reclaimed — it's the whole point, not fine print.
  • Protection built in. Battery-management circuitry for over-charge, over-discharge, short-circuit and temperature protection in every design.
  • We stay responsible. Acknowledging that cells are reclaimed doesn't shift safety onto you — statutory consumer rights are never waived, and any sold product would be backed by product-liability cover.

Re-Charge One is a concept product and is not yet in production. Renders are conceptual. Pre-orders are expressions of interest only — no payment is taken, no contract is formed, and estimated prices may change. Any future product using reclaimed cells would be clearly labelled and sold only after independent safety testing and certification.
